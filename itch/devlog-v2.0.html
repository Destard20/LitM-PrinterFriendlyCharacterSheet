A big update to the printer-friendly sheets:

  • Action Cards: a new page of 9 trading-card-size reminder cards (63 × 88 mm). Write an action and the tags you reach for when doing it, then keep it by your character sheet. A4 and Letter, printable or web-fillable.
  • Italian: every sheet is now also available in Italian on GitHub.
  • PDFs on this page: the character sheet and the action cards (A4 and Letter) as ready-to-print PDFs, plus a zip with the HTML files.
  • Weakness tags: the checkboxes next to weakness tags have been removed, on the character sheet and the action cards.
  • Same look everywhere: the sheets now carry their own free fonts, so they look the same on every computer and printer. Backgrounds print automatically too.

From now on the downloads here update automatically whenever the sheets change on GitHub.
